# TOS
A place to store the Terms of Service for butterdogco. 

# About

(or, why does this repo exist?)

This repo exists to provide a publicly available source from which the ButterDogCo terms of service can be accessed without the need to first access one of the products or services provided by ButterDogCo.

The contents of the repo, that is, the Terms of Service, exist because, as of the time of creation
  1) There is no unified Terms of Service for all provided services, and not all services have a Terms of Service.
  2) Where there is a Terms of Service, it either doesn't match up with the content of the service, contains contradictions, contains meme or joke clauses, or simply isn't extensive enough.
